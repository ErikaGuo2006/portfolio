About me

A FEW THINGS ABOUT ME

Atako Uesugi

My real name is Yilin / Erika, but I’d always rather be called Atako. You can also call me 321jump, ph3nol, or any name that can be shortened to sslx.

I work across illustration, game development, game art, and UI/UX. I’m drawn to simple compositions, crisp arrangements of points, lines, and shapes, and bold color contrasts—but somehow my style ended up on the cute side. These days, I’m stepping outside my comfort zone.

I like interaction design that weaves function naturally into visuals and storytelling.
